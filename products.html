Our fill

One product: certified organic tree cotton fill

Hand-picked tree cotton, de-seeded and cleaned on our own line and pressed into bales or bagged loose. Sold by the truckload or the container.

Specification

What is in the bale

Bolls are picked by hand as they split, dried on our yard, then de-seeded and cleaned - the fibre separated from seed and shell, de-dusted, and opened so it arrives lofted rather than matted. Nothing bought in is ever blended with it.

The ranges below are seasonal averages across our contracted acreage. Every lot ships with its own test report, so you buy against measured data rather than against this page.

Packing and quantities

How it ships

Pressed, numbered and warehoused until it is loaded. Everything below is per lot, and the figures on the offer are the ones on the bale.

The bale

Pressed bales of 80 kg, wrapped in cotton sheeting and numbered so the fill can be identified against its test report. Tree cotton is bulky for its weight - a bale is roughly 1.1 m³ compressed.

Bags, for smaller users

Grade A also goes out in 20 kg polywoven bags, which suit a toy or cushion workshop feeding a filling station by hand. Minimum 40 bags.

Container loads

About 6.5 MT in a 20 ft container and 14 MT in a 40 ft high cube. This fibre cubes out long before it weighs out, so quote your freight by volume.

Minimum order

800 kg for bagged Grade A, or one 20 ft container for baled. Below that a fibre trader is a better route than a mill.

Lead time

7–14 days in season, February to June. Out of season we ship from warehouse stock, usually within three weeks, subject to what is left of the crop.

Storage

Baled fill is kept dry, covered and away from ignition sources - loose tree cotton is readily flammable. We do not fumigate; stock moves within the season.

What comes with it

Documents, issued as standard

None of this is on request or at extra cost. If any of it is missing from your consignment, that is a mistake worth telling us about.

  • Test report for the lot - fibre length, bulk density, seed and shell, dust, moisture and colour.
  • Transaction certificate naming us as seller, you as buyer, and the certified organic status.
  • Weighbridge slip and bale list with net weight per bale.
  • Copies of our scope certificates, valid on the date of despatch.
  • Phytosanitary and fumigation-free declaration where your import rules need one.

Our certification and what it covers →

Seasonal testing

Beyond the per-lot bench test, we run three checks on the crop every season and pass the reports through unedited:

  • Fibre identification confirming the species
  • Pesticide residue screening
  • Restricted substances, to the list your market works to

If you are filling toys or nursery goods, tell us at enquiry. The finished article has to meet its own safety and flammability rules in your market, and those are tests on your product, not on our bale - but we will give you what you need from our end to support them.

Seed and shell

De-seeding separates fibre from seed. The seed goes back to the farms for sowing and local oil pressing, and the shell is composted on site - we do not trade either. Cleaned fibre is the only thing we sell.

Commercial terms

How we trade

Price

Quoted per MT against the season's farm-gate cost plus a published cleaning charge. Firm for 30 days.

Incoterms

Ex works at the mill, FOB or CIF to your named port. Domestic orders delivered by truck.

Payment

30% advance and balance against documents for new customers; irrevocable LC at sight or 30-day credit once a track record is established.

Inspection

Third-party pre-shipment inspection and independent testing welcome at your cost. Bales can be drawn at random.

Claims

Quality claims accepted within 30 days of arrival, re-tested against the retained material we keep from every lot.

Contracts

Forward contracts against the coming crop from July, which is how most of our repeat customers secure their volume.
