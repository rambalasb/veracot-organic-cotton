Our cotton

One product: certified organic tree cotton lint

Hand-picked Gossypium arboreum, roller-ginned on our own line and pressed into 170 kg bales. Sold by the truckload or the container.

Cotton is all we sell. No yarn, no fabric, no garments, no made-ups. We grow the crop and we gin it — that is the whole business. What you do with the fibre afterwards is your trade, and we have no interest in competing with our own customers for it.

Specification

What is in the bale

Seed cotton is picked by hand in several passes, dried on our yard and roller-ginned — never saw-ginned, which would break a short staple and lift the neps count. Nothing bought in is ever blended with it.

The ranges below are seasonal averages across our contracted acreage. Every lot ships with its own HVI report, so you buy against measured data rather than against this page.

Packing and quantities

How it ships

Pressed, numbered and warehoused until it is loaded. Everything below is per lot, and the figures on the offer are the ones on the bale.

The bale

Pressed bales of 170 kg, numbered so the cotton can be identified against its test report.

Container loads

About 82 bales — 14 MT — in a 20 ft container; about 170 bales — 29 MT — in a 40 ft high cube. We load at the gin.

Minimum order

14 MT — one 20 ft container or one truckload. Below that a lint trader is a better route than a gin.

Lead time

7–14 days in season, November to April. Out of season we ship from warehouse stock, usually within three weeks, subject to what is left of the crop.

Test data

The HVI report for the lot goes out with the offer, before you commit. You buy against measured figures, not against a description.

Storage

Baled lint is kept in a covered, ventilated warehouse at the gin. We do not fumigate; stock moves within the season.

What comes with it

Documents, issued as standard

None of this is on request or at extra cost. If any of it is missing from your consignment, that is a mistake worth telling us about.

  • HVI report for the lot — length, uniformity, strength, micronaire, trash and colour.
  • Transaction certificate naming us as seller, you as buyer, and the certified organic status.
  • Weighbridge slip and bale list with net weight per bale.
  • Copies of our scope certificates, valid on the date of despatch.
  • Phytosanitary and fumigation-free declaration where your import rules need one.

Our certification and what it covers →

Seasonal testing

Beyond the per-lot HVI, we run three checks on the crop every season and pass the reports through unedited:

  • GM screening by PCR
  • Fibre identification confirming the species
  • Pesticide residue screening

On tree cotton a positive GM result would not mean somebody planted modified seed — it would mean upland cotton got into the lot. That is exactly the thing worth catching.

Cottonseed

Ginning separates lint from seed. The seed goes back to the farms for sowing, feed and local oil pressing — we do not trade it. Lint is the only thing we sell.

Commercial terms

How we trade

Price

Quoted per candy or per MT against the season's farm-gate cost plus a published ginning charge. Firm for 30 days.

Incoterms

Ex works at the gin, FOB or CIF to your named port. Domestic orders delivered by truck.

Payment

30% advance and balance against documents for new customers; irrevocable LC at sight or 30-day credit once a track record is established.

Inspection

Third-party pre-shipment inspection and independent HVI testing welcome at your cost. Bales can be drawn at random.

Claims

Quality claims accepted within 30 days of arrival, re-tested against the retained material we keep from every lot.

Contracts

Forward contracts against the coming crop from July, which is how most of our repeat customers secure their volume.

Tell us the tonnage

Say what you are spinning or carding and how much you need, and we will come back with a price, a lead time and the HVI report for the lot.
