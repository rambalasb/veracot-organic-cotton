Visiting the gin

Buyers are welcome, and we would rather you came. The most useful window is December to March, when the gin is running and the groves are still in picking — you can see the crop, the gin line and the press in a day.

Give us two weeks' notice and we will send directions, arrange transport from wherever you land, and put together a grove visit and a walk through the gin.

Write to visits@veracot.example.

Before you travel

Ask the technical desk for the current season's HVI ranges first. If the fibre is clearly wrong for your process, we would rather tell you by email than have you make the trip.

Prefer to just send an email?

Write to the sales desk with the tonnage and what you are making. It reaches the same people who read the form.
